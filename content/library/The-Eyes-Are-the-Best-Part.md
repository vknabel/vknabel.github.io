Aversion, revenge, satisfaction, schadenfreude and a touch of disgust.
While story isn't deep, it catches you and is fun to read. Some parts are hard to swallow, but you get over them quickly.
This book is often classified as horror, but let's be honest: it isn't really.

I'd recomment this as a gripping book in between, that's definitely wort reading and will entertain you.

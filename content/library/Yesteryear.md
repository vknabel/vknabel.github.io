If you want to read a book that makes you feel lots of different emotions, then this is your pick. It includes everything from rage bait, confusion, amazement, some sadness. And you even feel a bit pity.

In here, you will follow a young woman, that turns into a trad-wife-influencer. And suddenly she finds herself in the glorified past and is confronted with reality.

Would definitely recommend!

This book digs deep into a wife's relationship, life and emotions. It starts with domestic violence, guides you to what lead to this situation and what follows.

The story unwinds slowly and carefully while still maintaining your attention.
It doesn't directly tell you the conclusions, but unravels everything piece by piece until you see, understand and _feel_ the conflict.

Really enjoyed reading this!

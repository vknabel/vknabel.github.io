Aborted reading after about a third. Even though I was enjoying the story, it was too slow paced and too much happened between the lines. You had to read everything carefully due to the writing style to get everything.

I am still curious about the book and might take it up in the future when my reading style changes. Not really suitable if you are regularly reading books on the train and in small chunks of time.

In here a facist party seize power in Ireland. It's told from the perspective of a woman with multiple children.
Interesting and current topic in our political situation with the rise of racist and facist parties around the world.

The story is told from the perspective from a mother. It's about hope, resignation and driven by the urge to turn back time.
From time to this reading this book makes you speachless, asking yourself why. But that's the point.
